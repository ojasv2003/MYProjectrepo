Full article examining the impact of India’s free trade agreements on logistics, supply chains, EXIM, trade lanes and related parameters, with landscape and future outlook.

## Core thesis

Tariffs open doors; logistics moves cargo through them. India’s recent FTAs (UAE CEPA, Australia ECTA, EFTA TEPA, UK CETA, EU package and others) reshape EXIM only to the extent that ports, corridors, customs digitisation, RoO/CoO compliance and multimodal networks keep pace.
