## Slide outline

1. **Title** — Supply Chains Under Uncertainty
2. **Landscape** — From fixed networks to constant motion
3. **Tariffs** — Trade policy rewired sourcing without ending imports
4. **Geopolitics** — Conflict risk as a standing condition
5. **Chokepoints** — Red Sea, Panama, and freight disruption
6. **Playbooks** — China+1, friendshoring, nearshoring, reshoring
7. **Data** — Confidence amid elevated barriers
8. **Implications** — Design for disruption
